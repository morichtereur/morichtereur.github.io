    Help and sources

    Flow

    1. Start: pick a function, choose a system landscape, say where transactional work sits today.
    2. Browse left to right: level 1, 2, 3, then the steps. The detail rail says where a process sits and what the rules suggest.
    3. Walk a process as a flow; drag a step between lanes to change where it is delivered.
    4. Send a process or a whole block to GBS, Local, CoE or Automate on the split view; or apply all suggestions.
    5. Read the assessment: split against the reference, gaps, handoffs, what the rules would move. Compare with APQC; export, or save the project to share.

    Sources

    Reference taxonomies. Nine finance clusters to level 4 - Record to Report, Invoice to Pay, Invoice to Cash, Acquire to Retire, Intercompany, Plan to Report, Treasury and Cash, Tax, Hire to Retire - this tool's own reference design, checked against APQC PCF 7.4 category 9.0 (groups 9.1 to 9.10) and SAP S/4HANA Best Practices scope items. Plan to Report, Treasury and Cash, Tax and Hire to Retire, and the revenue recognition block of Invoice to Cash, are written from APQC's element list, IAS 12, IFRS 15, the OECD transfer pricing guidelines and common practice; every level-3 process names its source. A design view written to be argued with, not a survey.

    Assessment. One screen for the open taxonomy - split against the reference, gaps, handoffs, what the rules would move, what the systems hold back - with every process linked into the flow. It prints.

    Your systems. Twenty-three answers grouped by function, with presets for a whole landscape. An automation suggestion names the system it needs; without it the suggestion falls back to GBS, and the mix strip says how many are held back.

    What happens to a sheet you load. Structural repairs (numbering, missing parents, rows numbered under the wrong parent, a title row), names cleaned (routings and conditions out, spelling, abbreviations written out), steps without a destination placed by a rule that is shown (dashed edge), and a process left without steps beside a twin that has them takes the twin's steps with the noun swapped. Everything of that kind is marked on the page and in the export.

    End-to-end clusters. The start screen uses the cluster names common to ERP and consulting practice - order to cash, procure to pay, record to report, acquire to retire, plan to report, treasury, tax, hire to retire - as in Microsoft's business-process guidance and Deloitte on fit-for-purpose end-to-end solutions. The reference holds the finance part of each; the card says which.

    Operating model. The tiering language - transactional shared services, centres of expertise, retained organisation - follows the global business services literature, e.g. The Hackett Group and Deloitte. The rules on this page are a design view built on that language; no survey figure is quoted.

    Interface. The column browser is a Miller-columns view, the pattern behind the Finder's column view since 1986. Miller columns
