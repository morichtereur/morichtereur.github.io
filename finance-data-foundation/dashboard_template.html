The expensive errors are the ones that don't crash

Three entities export their ledgers the way ERP jobs actually do — German dates and decimal commas from Hamburg, złoty from Kraków, and one extract job that ran twice. Concatenate the files and two of those problems crash your query, so they get fixed. The other two parse cleanly and inflate revenue in silence. This page is the governed alternative: one staged model, every metric defined once, every reconciliation tested on every build. Everything below is read from the built marts, every chart answers the cursor, and the two switchable exhibits recompute as you go.

The verdict, on the FY2025 books

overstatement against the governed figure — złoty read as euros, plus one extract loaded twice
gap between three accepted DSO formulas on the same ledger, December 2025
checks green on this build — reconciliations and schema tests; a red build ships nothing

Neither silent error is exotic. Currency left unconverted and a file loaded twice are among the most ordinary failures in consolidation — which is the argument: the defence is not a more careful analyst, it is a layer where the repair is written once, and a test that proves it happened. Every number on this page comes out of that layer.
